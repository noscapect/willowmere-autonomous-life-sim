# Roadmap

1. **Foundation (complete):** deterministic time, needs, semantic objects, typed validated actions, memory events, and autonomous inhabitants.
2. **Situated people (complete):** A* navigation, river crossing, local perception, knowledge discovery, interaction positions, short plans, and deterministic exploration.
3. **A watchable world (current):** read-only 3D renderer, organic decorative terrain, time-aware atmosphere, procedural settlement geometry, articulated residents, camera, and 3D selection.
4. **A working settlement:** ownership, occupations, economy, inventory logistics, crafting, farming, construction, and agent-modified terrain.
5. **Long lives:** skills/learning, families, culture, illness/injury, seasonal ecology, and dynamic settlements.

At every phase, LLMs may propose goals and plans but are never a world-state authority.
